**Description** (1,827/4,000)
```
Run a fulfillment floor on a space station.

Space trucks dock and unload cargo. You take it from there: stack pallets, rack the cartons, pick orders, pack them, sort them and ship them out to the stars. Every box that leaves the outbound dock pays, and every payday grows your depot.

THE FLOOR
• 01 Inbound dock: grab pallets as the dock crew builds them
• 02 Bulk storage: store pallets, then break them down into cartons
• 03 + 04 Pick rack: restock from the back, pick orders from the front
• 05 Pack line: drop totes on the belt, pack at the table, take the cart
• 06 Sorter: a robot arm sorts boxes into bins. Forklift the fullest one
• 07 Outbound dock: load the space truck and collect the cash

GROW YOUR DEPOT
• Hire a crew: PALLY the pallet-jack bot, a picker, a packer, a restocker, a sorter and a forklift driver keep the floor moving
• Build a taller rack, bigger trucks, AUTO-PAY and EXPRESS ORDERS
• Upgrade your speed, carry capacity, profit and crew
• Power Pack: 2X packing and 2X pay for a minute
• Peak season rushes: 2X orders, 1.5X pay
• Open new depots, from LUNA DEPOT to MARS HUB to EUROPA PORT, where every box pays 4X more

COME BACK EVERY DAY
• A 7-day reward streak that grows with your depot, with a 5-minute Power Pack on day 7
• Your crew keeps shipping while you're away: collect up to 3 hours of offline earnings
• Optional reminders when the dock is full or your next reward is ready

MADE FOR PHONES
• One-thumb controls: drag anywhere to walk, stand at a station to work it
• A guided first shift teaches the whole loop
• Plays offline, no account needed
• Optional videos give you bonuses, or buy NO ADS once to get every reward instantly (restore it any time from Settings)
• Sound, music and vibration each have their own switch

Clock in, boss. The galaxy is waiting for its packages.
```

## App Review information
**Notes for the reviewer:**
```
ORBIT DEPOT is a single-player offline game; no account or login is needed.

In-app purchase: "Remove Ads" (remove_ads, non-consumable) is offered on the title screen (NO ADS button) and in the pause menu. Restore Purchases is under Settings on the title screen and in the pause menu (the II button, top right during play).

Ads: optional rewarded videos only (the Power Pack, doubling offline earnings and the daily reward). No ad ever plays without the player tapping a button. Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown once at first launch, after Google's consent form where it applies.

Notifications: the game asks for notification permission only after the player claims their first daily reward, and only schedules local reminders (no push server).
```
**Sign-in required:** No
